01

Traditional Art

Leaving Certificate Art

Higher Level · H1

My Leaving Certificate Art project explored identity, perception and the influence of contemporary visual culture through portraiture, mixed media, observational drawing and visual research.

Development work combined drawing, painting, collage, typography and collected visual references. The project allowed me to experiment with composition, colour and different methods of representing people before developing the final piece.

Process

Research, experimentation & development.

The project developed through visual research sheets, observational studies and experimentation with portraiture, colour, collage and mixed media.

02

Technical Visualisation

Drawing & CAD

Design & Communication Graphics introduced me to technical drawing, product visualisation and CAD, developing an understanding of form, proportion, construction and communicating three-dimensional objects.

The work combined freehand product sketches with computer-aided modelling in SolidWorks, providing an early foundation for the 3D and digital design work I later developed at university.

Project Documentation

Design development.

A short overview of the wider Design & Communication Graphics project, documenting technical studies and development work across the project.

Continuing into University

From technical drawing to digital prototyping.

I continued exploring three-dimensional design at university through CAD and physical-computing projects. This included developing concepts for LightLink, a touchscreen-controlled smart lighting hub.

The project explored product form, ergonomics and component placement, including a protective enclosure for an ESP32 and a larger lightbulb-inspired fixture.

03

Moving Image

Digital Video

Digital Video Fundamentals introduced another dimension to my visual practice through moving image, composition, sequencing, sound and editing.

This project explored visual storytelling through a deliberately edited sequence combining environmental, architectural and studio imagery.

04

Sound & Multimedia

Digital Audio

Alongside visual media, I developed foundational audio-production skills using REAPER and university mixing facilities.

My final project involved creating a cohesive multitrack mix using drums, bass, lead vocals, piano, choir and Rhodes. I worked with EQ, compression, reverb and stereo width while considering clarity, balance and the relationship between individual elements in the mix.

REAPER Multitrack Mixing EQ Compression Reverb Stereo Imaging

Reflection

Working across traditional and digital media has shaped how I approach design today — combining visual judgement, experimentation and technical skills rather than relying on a single medium or tool.

These projects represent different stages of my creative development, from observational drawing and traditional art to technical visualisation, CAD, moving image and digital production.
